
CollabNGrow --- Brand Guidelines

1. Brand Identity

Brand Name: CollabNGrow

Brand Promise: Connection, collaboration, growth and meaningful
relationships.

Primary Brand Colours: White + Rose Pink

The visual identity should feel:

Warm

Human

Positive

Trustworthy

Energetic

Modern

Community-oriented

Premium without feeling corporate

Clean and easy to use

The product should communicate people coming together, creating value
and growing together.

2. Visual References

The supplied reference screenshots and logo are the visual source
material for this brand system.

The implementation agent must inspect and use the supplied assets rather
than replacing them with generic alternatives.

Supplied visual references

CollabNGrow application/dashboard screenshot

CollabNGrow logo

CollabNGrow website/footer screenshot

The logo includes a white circular mark on a strong rose-pink
background, with a handshake/community-growth visual and the CollabNGrow

3. Core Colour Palette

Primary Rose Pink

HEX: #E0023F

Use this as the principal brand colour.

Recommended uses:

Primary buttons

Active navigation

Important calls to action

Links

Brand highlights

Key headings where appropriate

Selected states

Icons

Important interface accents

Deep Rose / Supporting Pink

HEX: #C11C45

Use as a darker supporting rose for:

Headers

Sidebar backgrounds

Hero sections

Gradient endpoints

Stronger emphasis

Hover/active states

White

HEX: #FFFFFF

White is a major part of the identity.

Use it for:

Main page backgrounds

Cards

Navigation text

Buttons placed on rose backgrounds

Spacious content areas

Clean negative space

Soft Rose Tint

Use very light rose tints derived from the primary rose for:

Card backgrounds

Selected states

Subtle section backgrounds

Notifications

Decorative shapes

Do not use saturated pink for every surface.

4. Colour Philosophy

The dominant visual relationship should be:

Rose Pink + White + generous whitespace

Avoid creating a rainbow-like interface.

The brand should remain recognisable even if a user sees the interface
from a distance.

Preferred hierarchy

White --- primary canvas

Rose Pink --- brand/action

Deep Rose --- emphasis/navigation

Neutral dark text --- readability

Soft rose tints --- supporting surfaces

Neutral colours may be used for accessibility and readability, but the
overall identity must remain Rose Pink + White.

5. Gradients

Gradients may be used selectively.

Preferred direction:

Primary Rose → Deep Rose

Use gradients for:

Hero banners

Major call-to-action sections

Premium feature panels

Login/authentication surfaces

Completion moments

Do NOT use gradients on every component.

The gradient should enhance hierarchy rather than become the entire
visual language.

6. Typography

Use a clean, modern sans-serif typeface.

Typography should prioritise:

Excellent readability

Clear hierarchy

Comfortable line height

Strong mobile readability

Recommended hierarchy:

Display / Hero

Bold or semibold.

Used for:

Main page introductions

Major statements

Key experience moments

H1

Bold / semibold.

H2

Semibold.

H3

Semibold or medium.

Body

Regular.

Keep paragraphs comfortably readable rather than overly dense.

Small / Supporting text

Use sparingly and maintain sufficient contrast.

Do not use decorative fonts for the main application interface.

7. Logo

The supplied CollabNGrow logo is the primary brand mark.

Use the supplied logo asset wherever possible.

Logo rules

Do not distort the logo.

Do not stretch it disproportionately.

Do not rotate it.

Do not change its colours.

Do not add effects that alter the original identity.

Maintain clear space around the logo.

Prefer the supplied high-quality asset over recreating the logo in
CSS.

Use an appropriate favicon/app icon derived from the supplied logo
where required.

Logo placement

Suitable locations include:

Login page

Navigation

PWA launch experience

Admin dashboard

Footer

Completion/thank-you screen

8. Navigation

The supplied application screenshot demonstrates a strong rose
navigation/sidebar.

For desktop:

Rose sidebar

White logo/brand treatment

Clear navigation labels

White active-state surface where appropriate

Rose/pink accent for selected elements

For mobile:

Collapse navigation appropriately.

Use a clean mobile menu or bottom navigation where appropriate.

Preserve the same visual hierarchy.

Navigation should feel simple and uncluttered.

9. Buttons

Primary button:

Rose Pink background

White text

Rounded corners

Strong readable label

Comfortable touch target

Secondary button:

White or neutral background

Rose border/text

On rose backgrounds:

White buttons with rose text are encouraged where this improves
contrast and hierarchy.

Buttons should feel friendly rather than aggressive.

Avoid excessive pill-shaped buttons unless they fit the specific
component.

10. Cards

Cards should be:

Clean

Spacious

Light

Easy to scan

Preferred treatment:

White background

Subtle border

Very subtle shadow when necessary

Rounded corners

Comfortable internal padding

Use soft rose-tinted backgrounds for selected or highlighted cards.

Avoid heavy shadows.

11. Forms

Forms should feel calm and approachable.

Input fields should have:

White or very light background

Clear border

Rounded corners

Strong focus state

Clearly visible labels

Comfortable touch target

Focus state:

Rose Pink border/ring

Error state:

Clear accessible error treatment

Do not rely only on colour to communicate an error.

12. Experience Design

CollabNGrow experiences should feel human.

Use generous whitespace.

Avoid:

Dense dashboards

Excessive borders

Tiny text

Too many controls on one screen

Unnecessary animations

Generic SaaS styling

The user should always understand:

Where they are

What is happening

What they need to do

Why it matters

What happens next

13. Photography and Imagery

When using imagery, prefer images that communicate:

Human connection

Collaboration

Conversation

Growth

Community

Trust

Achievement

Positive relationships

Avoid generic corporate stock photography where possible.

Avoid overly staged business imagery.

The brand is about people and relationships, not just business
transactions.

14. Icons

Use a consistent modern icon family throughout the product.

Icons should generally be:

Simple

Minimal

Easy to understand

Consistent in stroke/weight

Rose Pink can be used as the primary icon accent.

Do not mix multiple unrelated icon styles.

15. Motion

Animations should be subtle and purposeful.

Good uses:

Page transitions

Button feedback

Loading states

Progress indicators

Completion moments

Modal transitions

Avoid:

Excessive bouncing

Distracting motion

Long transitions

Animations that slow down the experience

Respect reduced-motion accessibility preferences.

16. PWA Experience

The PWA should feel like a real CollabNGrow application rather than a
website opened in a browser.

Use the supplied logo for:

App icon

Splash/launch treatment where supported

Favicon

PWA manifest icons

The visual experience should remain consistent between:

Desktop

Mobile browser

Installed PWA

17. Admin Dashboard

The admin dashboard may be more information-dense than the participant
experience.

However, it must still follow the CollabNGrow brand.

Use:

White main workspace

Rose navigation/branding

Rose primary actions

Clean tables

Clear status indicators

Generous spacing

The admin dashboard should feel like a professional control centre, not
a developer console.

18. Authentication Screens

Authentication screens should feel trustworthy.

Use:

White space

CollabNGrow logo

Rose primary CTA

Clear explanation

Minimal distractions

For Google authentication:

Continue with Google

should be visually clear without making the authentication process feel
intimidating.

19. Empty States

Empty states should be friendly and useful.

Avoid:

Nothing here.

Prefer a concise explanation plus a next action.

Example:

Your journey hasn't started yet.

When you're ready, begin your reflection.

[ Begin ]

20. Error States

Error messages should be:

Human

Clear

Actionable

Non-technical

Do not expose internal Firebase, API, Firestore or server errors to
normal users.

Instead of:

FirebaseError: permission-denied

Use:

We couldn't complete that request. Please try again.

Where relevant, provide a support option.

21. Tone Of Voice

CollabNGrow communicates like a thoughtful human guide.

The tone should be:

Warm

Encouraging

Clear

Respectful

Confident

Human

Avoid:

Corporate jargon

Overly formal language

Excessive exclamation marks

Artificial motivational language

Manipulative language

Condescending language

22. Content Principles

Write for humans.

Prefer:

Let's begin.

over:

Initiate assessment.

Prefer:

Your journey is ready.

over:

Assessment instance successfully initialized.

Prefer:

We couldn't verify your account.

over:

Authentication failure.

23. Responsive Design

Design mobile-first.

The application must work well on:

Mobile phones

Tablets

Laptops

Large desktop screens

Do not simply shrink the desktop interface.

On small screens:

Reduce visual density

Stack controls

Increase touch targets

Simplify navigation

Preserve comfortable reading width

24. Accessibility

The brand must never compromise accessibility.

Maintain:

Strong text contrast

Visible focus states

Keyboard navigation

Semantic HTML

Accessible labels

Screen-reader-friendly controls

Adequate touch targets

Reduced-motion support

Rose Pink should not be used as the sole indicator of state.

25. Design Tokens

Create a central theme/token system.

Example:

:root {
  --brand-primary: #E0023F;
  --brand-primary-dark: #C11C45;
  --brand-white: #FFFFFF;

  --text-primary: #1F1F1F;
  --text-secondary: #666666;

  --surface: #FFFFFF;
  --surface-soft: #FFF5F7;

  --border: #E9E9E9;

  --radius-sm: 8px;
  --radius-md: 12px;
  --radius-lg: 18px;
}

These are starting implementation tokens. The supplied logo/reference
assets should remain the visual authority.

Do not scatter raw colours throughout components.

26. Do / Don't

DO

Use Rose Pink and White prominently.

Preserve generous whitespace.

Use the supplied logo.

Create strong visual hierarchy.

Keep interfaces human and approachable.

Use subtle rounded corners.

Use rose gradients selectively.

Make primary actions obvious.

Keep mobile experience excellent.

Maintain accessibility.

DON'T

Use arbitrary colours without a reason.

Introduce unrelated brand colours.

Overuse gradients.

Overuse shadows.

Use generic stock imagery when supplied brand imagery is
appropriate.

Make every component bright pink.

Create overly dense screens.

Use tiny text.

Distort the logo.

Turn the product into a generic SaaS template.

28. Passion Analyzer Brand Adaptation

The Passion Analyzer is a CollabNGrow experience.

It should therefore feel like a natural part of the CollabNGrow
ecosystem.

However, it is a deeper reflective experience than the networking
dashboard.

Use the same:

Logo

Rose + White palette

Typography principles

Button language

Spacing

Rounded surfaces

Brand tone

But allow the Passion Analyzer to feel:

More reflective

More spacious

More intimate

More editorial

More thoughtful

Avoid making it look like a conventional survey.

The participant should feel that they are entering a meaningful personal
journey.

29. Visual Priority For The Passion Analyzer

The interface should prioritise:

Question

↓

Reflection

↓

Writing

↓

Progress

↓

Insight

The answer field should be the visual centre of each exercise screen.

Do not crowd the question with unnecessary navigation.

30. Completion Experience

Completion should feel meaningful.

The final screen may use:

The CollabNGrow logo

Rose gradient

White typography

A concise personal statement

Key themes

Personal priorities

Next actions

Do not turn the result into a clinical "score".

This experience is about self-understanding, not judging the
participant.

31. Source Of Truth

When implementing the product:

brand_guidelines.md defines visual identity.

exercise.md defines the exercise content.

The technical/master specification defines system behaviour.

Firebase configuration defines authentication/data infrastructure.

Supplied image/logo assets define the actual visual brand
references.

If these documents contain ambiguity, preserve the intent rather than
inventing unrelated functionality.

32. Final Brand Test

Before shipping, ask:

Does this look like CollabNGrow?

Does the Rose + White identity immediately register?

Does the supplied logo feel naturally integrated?

Does the interface feel human?

Is there enough whitespace?

Is the primary action obvious?

Does the experience feel premium without being excessive?

Would a participant feel comfortable writing deeply personal reflections here?

Does the Passion Analyzer feel like a natural CollabNGrow experience?

If the answer to any of these is no, refine the UI before shipping.
